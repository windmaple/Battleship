Three AI Ranks & Three Combat Doctrines

From classic single-shot duels to multi-weapon Tactical Ops, customize every engagement or hand off the console in 2-Player Classified Hotseat mode.

FLEET ADMIRAL AI

Combinatorial Probability Matrix

Evaluates every valid horizontal and vertical placement of remaining ships across all 100 cells, weighting un-sunk hull hits and checkerboard parity to compute optimal strikes—and lets you toggle the same live sensor overlay on your own HUD.

3 RULE VARIANTS

Classic, Salvo & Tactical Ops

Play traditional 1-shot Classic, dynamic Salvo where your shots per turn match your surviving fleet count, or Tactical Ops featuring 3×3 Active Sonar Pings, 1×3 Harpoon Airstrikes, and 5-cell Tomahawk Cross Barrages.

PROCEDURAL WAV ENGINE

Zero-Dependency Naval Audio

Synthesizes 44.1 kHz PCM waveforms directly in memory—generating resonant sonar pings, compressed-air torpedo launches, multi-stage hull detonations, sinking klaxons, and hydro splashes without external audio assets.
